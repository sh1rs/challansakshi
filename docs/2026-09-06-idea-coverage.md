# Original idea coverage — 6 September 2026

This map checks the supplied `pasted-text.txt` discussion, from reducing the effort of challenging a ₹100 challan through the broader mobility assistant, against the current workspace. Local preparation and continuity now cover much of the proposed starting experience. The full service that navigates official systems, completes authorized actions and returns verified outcomes remains partial.

**Local** means implemented in the current workspace. **Needs provider** means an implementation exists but requires configuration and live validation. **Synthetic** means the behavior is demonstrated only with fictional data/services. **Remaining** means the requested real capability or outcome evidence is not implemented or established. A row can have more than one status. These are capability distinctions, not a deployment statement. The [7 September release record](./2026-09-07-mobility-live-release.md) tracks publication of these features. The [next-actions report](./2026-09-06-mobility-next-actions-status.md) covers the latest local continuation and its verification status; the [continuity report](./2026-09-06-mobility-continuity-status.md) preserves the preceding checkpoint. Local records expire 90 days after their last relevant save and are cleaned up on a subsequent read or action; expiry is not a background deletion guarantee for a closed browser.

The original connected promise is therefore covered through **guest intake → reviewed preparation and visit pack → optional local/account continuity → personal next-step agenda → manual official handoff → reviewed citizen report and source follow-up**, with a separate synthetic demonstration of execution and recovery. The gap to **authorized official execution → verified acknowledgement → source-connected monitoring** remains explicit and requires real access plus outcome validation.

The [continuity report](./2026-09-06-mobility-continuity-status.md) retains its completed verification checkpoint, including the exact-preview manual form-copy panel. The later [next-actions report](./2026-09-06-mobility-next-actions-status.md) tracks agenda, acknowledgement, visit-preparation and manual session-clear work; earlier counts are not verification of this continuation. None enables a real portal adapter, background monitoring or paid provider. Real-phone voice quality, translation review and citizen outcomes remain separate validation work.
