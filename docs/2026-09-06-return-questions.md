## Suggested first walkthrough

For a practice run, use fictional information only. You can paste this task, review the three proposed details, then set the case title to **Practice case — fictional**:

```text
I want to review a fictional challan. Vehicle KA01ZZ1234. Reference: DEMO2026. Issuing authority: Karnataka Traffic Police.
```

This is a test input, not a real notice or official record. Saving it is still an explicit choice; delete the practice case afterward.

1. Open `/mobility`, describe a task with a registration or explicitly labelled reference, and review the recognized details before making the plan.
2. Add or reuse reviewed information, choose **Check this case**, and follow its single next action. Save only after the private-device review.
3. For the appointment exercise, create a separate fictional licence-renewal preparation. Under **Appointment and visit pack**, enter a practice booking within the next 30 local calendar days, a fictional venue and one practice instruction per line. Expand the optional checklist, choose reviewed lines and preview the exact visit pack before approving its download. Extra case facts/reference stay out unless selected; the separate calendar preview excludes venue and instructions.
4. Save that preparation deliberately. In **Your next steps**, inspect why its appointment appears and use the card to reopen it. The agenda also combines due personal/source checks, cases marked for attention and entered document dates; none is a verified official status or legal deadline.
5. Open **Review text from an acknowledgement** and paste fictional labelled text such as `Reference: PRACTICE-ACK-2026`. Name its practice source, select or correct a reading, choose your own reported progress and approve the exact timeline note. Check any reference replacement deliberately. The report changes only the working case; save separately to retain it. “Paid” or “disposed” in pasted text does not choose progress.
6. Choose **Clear this session**, reload, and confirm the neutral screen remains until **Start a new session**. This clears unsaved forms and approvals in this tab; saved practice records remain available in the new session. It does not sign out an account or clear other tabs, downloads or clipboard contents.

Other local exercises remain available when useful:

- Export a short brief or a passphrase-protected portable case, then import the encrypted file as a new unsaved copy. Record a source observation and review its personal calendar reminder; `/reply-review` can prepare a separate follow-up case from a reviewed reply note.
- Add a fictional date under **Document expiry organiser** and review its personal reminder. A licence date can start a separate renewal preparation whose carried details need confirmation. With two saved practice cases, use **Connected life-event plans** to link them explicitly while preserving their separate histories.
- Use **Copy details for an official form** to select confirmed details and approve an exact copy preview. To try recovery, edit the same saved case in two tabs, save one, then compare and select changes from the other working draft.
