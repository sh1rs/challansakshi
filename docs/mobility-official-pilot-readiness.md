# Official mobility pilot readiness

**Public-source review: 6 September 2026.** This is preparation for selecting one official pilot. No sign-in, personal-record lookup, form submission, credential creation or provider approval was performed. The current app prepares cases and records user-reported progress; it has no approved government execution or DigiLocker connector. ## DigiLocker and API Setu prerequisites

The [DigiLocker resource centre](https://apisetu.gov.in/digilocker) publishes requester specifications, partner onboarding and terms. Access to those documents does not confer requester status or permission to automate another government website.

- **Organization and approval:** the [partner onboarding SOP, dated 5 June 2024](https://cf-media.api-setu.in/resources/Partners-SOP.pdf), calls for a registered organization, functional service website, official-domain email and an authorized representative. It describes verification of the organization and use case, agreement acceptance, technical integration, testing and launch approval. It also says temporary access solely for testing will not be entertained. Prepare the entity/representative evidence, a specific citizen journey and demo/test data. Confirm which digital-signature and infrastructure requirements apply to the proposed requester role with the onboarding team; this public SOP covers issuers as well as requesters.
- **API-specific permission:** the guide linked as SOP V2 on the [API Setu SOP page](https://apisetu.gov.in/sop) describes consumer approval followed by publisher subscription approval. The [linked guide](https://cdn.apisetu.gov.in/portal/assets/sop-apisetu-v1.pdf), pp. 4–11, asks for a use case and an API permission/authorization letter from the publisher; submitting a subscription does not itself enable live access. Its filename contains `v1` despite the page's V2 label. Confirm the applicable workflow and API version during onboarding. No eChallan read/write API entitlement was established by this review.
- **Consent and document access:** the [Requester API specification v1.12](https://cf-media.api-setu.in/resources/Requester-APISpecification-V1_12.pdf), pp. 3–9, documents a user-facing OAuth authorization page, exact registered callback, state, PKCE S256, approved document types, granted scopes, consent expiry and token revocation. It distinguishes issuer-provided documents from self-uploaded files (pp. 12–17). Build only the approved document scope; fetching a licence or registration document does not submit a grievance or prove a challan's current disposition.
- **Retention and operating obligations:** the [Requester Terms, updated 2 June 2025](https://cf-media.api-setu.in/resources/DigiLocker-Terms-of-User-Requester-june-2025.pdf), §§4(d), 6(b), restrict caching/storage and reproduction outside the transaction except the stated permitted/legal circumstances. They require informed consent, a privacy notice, incident notification within 24 hours and an annual compliance audit through a CERT-In-empaneled auditor (§§4, 12–15); scraping and unauthorized endpoint use are prohibited (§5). **Do not assume our existing local storage, 90-day account case retention, helper snapshots, exports or optional AI processing are permitted for DigiLocker-derived content.** Obtain a documented determination of allowed fields, duration and onward use before enabling any of those paths. Review the current terms again at onboarding; this note does not determine legal eligibility or exceptions.

## Inputs needed before implementing a live adapter

1. **One selected service and issuing authority:** state, department/commissionerate, challan versus notice, current official portal, and whether the record has a court-related status. Choose from the actual notice/record; an OCR-extracted location remains unconfirmed until reviewed.
2. **A consenting pilot participant and scoped task:** the exact record and action they want, which facts/documents may be used, and who performs each step. Request only what that selected flow requires; do not collect credentials or OTPs in the app.
3. **Authority and provider decisions:** a named organizational contact, approved use case, any publisher permission letter, approved API/document types, environments, quotas, charges and written terms for delegated or automated actions. Public accessibility is not automation permission.
4. **The operating organization:** registered entity details, authorized representative, official email/domain, privacy and retention owner, security/incident contact, and resources for required audits. Credentials should be provisioned privately only after approval.
5. **A definition of success:** choose whether the pilot demonstrates a document retrieval, an accepted submission, payment reconciliation or a subsequent official decision. Record those as separate outcomes.

## Integration work after those inputs are settled

These are proposed engineering gates, not claims that the providers have approved this design:

- Verify the selected live flow with a participant controlling official sign-in, CAPTCHA, OTP and any final submission/payment. Record the current fields, limits, required consent, failure states and receipt format. Keep manual handoff until the access conditions and the adapter's allowed scope are established; obtain publisher approval where the API or service requires it.
- For an approved API, implement server-side credentials, exact account/task binding, callback/state validation, request bounds, approved scopes and consent expiry/revocation. Verify the current provider specification instead of treating the public examples as a production contract.
- Preserve source identity, issuer/document type, retrieval time and uncertainty; distinguish user uploads, parsed readings and issuer responses. A matching source hash detects the same bytes, not document authenticity or official acceptance.
- Enforce the agreed retention at every path: browser state, account copy, helper access, exports, model context, logs and backups. Test deletion, expiry and revoked access with delayed responses and account switching.
- Handle timeouts and ambiguous submissions without claiming success or blindly repeating a write. Reconcile by the provider's supported reference/status mechanism; preserve the last confirmed observation and show when a newer check failed.
- Use approved test data/environment first. Then run a separately authorized live pilot, with redacted evidence and explicit separation between fixture results and real provider responses.

## Evidence required for each completion claim

The following is the proposed product acceptance standard. Store evidence only where the approved retention rules permit it; keep public reports redacted.

| Claim | Minimum evidence | What remains unproven |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preparation complete | Citizen-reviewed input snapshot, source/uncertainty markers and the exact prepared text. | Official receipt, acceptance or decision. |
| DigiLocker document retrieved | Approved requester/client scope and consent; successful provider response matched to the selected user, issuer, document type/URI and retrieval time. Distinguish issuer-provided from self-uploaded material. | Current challan status, validity beyond what the issuer states, or grievance submission. |
| Official submission accepted | Official acknowledgement/reference tied to the exact authority, task and submitted version, plus submission time and the provider's returned status. A replayable status/receipt check where the provider supports one. | A favorable decision or final resolution. |
| Payment recorded officially | Official transaction/receipt reference matched to the challan, amount, payment date and successful official status; resolve any conflicting pending/failed state. | A bank debit or payment-page redirect alone is insufficient. |
| Official case resolved | Dated decision or final status from the competent authority, matched to the original reference and stating the actual outcome. Preserve the difference between accepted, rejected, withdrawn and resolved. | A helper proposal, generated draft, user-entered status or successful HTTP response without the decision. |

If only a participant's account of completion is available, label it **user-reported**. If an official receipt is uploaded but not independently checked, label it **receipt supplied; verification pending**. A hash, OCR match, mock API or local browser test cannot upgrade either label to verified official completion.
