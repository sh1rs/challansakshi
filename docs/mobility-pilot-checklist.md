Use this after choosing one state and one official service. The current build establishes local preparation and tested integration boundaries; this record is for the first real journey. Do not mark a gate passed from a synthetic portal, stubbed model, source review or another jurisdiction.

## Measure comfort, not feature count

Start with a few willing people who have a recent relevant task. Have each person use their own documents and accounts. Keep the observation record limited to task-level measurements unless separate consent covers anything more.

The optional effort notebook records a local active-time estimate, self-reported repeated entries and help needs, plus the person's stated outcome. Its export contains no case text or identifier. Record these alongside:

- Whether the person understood the next action without explanation.
- Where the person corrected a reading or could not resolve it.
- Whether the task ended in an acknowledgement, a clear blocker, a useful prepared handoff or abandonment.
- What official evidence supports any completion claim.
- Whether a return visit resumed the right place without re-entering details.

Do not convert a small convenience sample into a general success rate or time-saving claim. Document the tasks, conditions and failures. Prioritize the most repeated observed obstacle before adding another service family.

## Expansion decision

Proceed to a licence journey in the same state after the initial service has a supported access path, a case-matched real outcome, a verified recovery path and a usable deletion process. If official access remains unavailable, keep the citizen-controlled preparation and manual handoff useful and accurately labelled.
